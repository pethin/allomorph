ALLOMORPH: 34" STANDARD JAZZ BASS EDITION

Transform your 34" Standard Jazz Bass into 24 legendary active, vintage, modern, and acoustic pickup configurations.


OVERVIEW

Allomorph turns your 34" Standard Jazz Bass into a versatile tonal chameleon. Instead of modeling an amplifier or speaker cabinet, Allomorph sits at the very beginning of your signal chain (Block 1 on the Darkglass Anagram) to reshape the actual sound, pickup response, and feel of your instrument.

With this pack, your 34" Standard Jazz Bass can instantly deliver:
• The iconic scoop of vintage '60s Jazz Basses and aggressive punch of Music Man StingRays
• Modern active slap preamps, boutique P/MM hybrids, and cutting progressive rock clank
• Warm Motown flatwound thump, deep reggae dub sub-bass, and pristine modern ceramic punch
• Acoustic upright double bass resonance for jazz and acoustic sessions
• Pure studio buffer clarity or organic vintage tube-friendly passive warmth

Calibrated specifically for the 34" Standard Jazz Bass. Plug in, select a model, and hear your 34" Standard Jazz Bass transform into a completely different instrument in real time with zero latency.


RECOMMENDED SIGNAL CHAIN

[34" Standard Jazz Bass]
  -> [Block 1: Allomorph Pickup Twin] (Input / Pickup Front-End)
  -> [Block 2: Amp / Drive / Preamp]  (Darkglass B7K, SVT, B-15, etc.)
  -> [Block 3: Speaker Cabinet IR]    (8x10, 4x10, 1x15, etc.)
  -> [FOH / Audio Interface / DAW]

Why Block 1? Overdrive pedals and amps react directly to the resonant character of your pickups. Putting Allomorph first ensures downstream pedals and amp models distort the authentic growl of a Jazz bridge pickup, the clank of a StingRay, or the scoop of dual single-coils, rather than a generic DI signal.


QUICK INSTRUMENT SETUP

For each voicing, set your bass's physical pickup controls to the recommended bracketed setting [Neck], [Bridge], [Parallel]:

• [Neck]: Select Jazz Neck Single-Coil
• [Bridge]: Select 60s Jazz Bridge Single-Coil
• [Parallel]: Select Jazz Neck + Bridge Parallel
• Master Tone Knob: 100% (Wide Open across all models)
• Physical Volume Knob: 100% (Wide Open)

Plug straight in. All pickup positioning, pot loading, tone capacitor shaping, and active preamp EQs are handled automatically by the models.


THE 24 DIGITAL TWIN VOICINGS


Precision Bass & Tone Shaper Family

01. Precision Vintage [Neck] v4.1.1
The gold standard: a vintage 1962 Precision Bass with tone wide open. Warm, woody low-mids, open harmonic bloom, and organic touch sensitivity that sits perfectly in any mix.

02. Precision Active [Neck] v4.1.1
Modern active ceramic split-coil P-bass tone with 2-band preamp. Punchy, articulate, and wideband with authoritative low-end weight and bright transient clarity—built for modern rock, gospel, and mix cutting authority.

03. Precision Mids [Neck] v4.1.1
Vintage P-bass with a modern vocal low-mid punch. Cuts finger clatter and high-end sizzle while keeping a muscular, forward presence in a busy mix.

04. Precision Warm [Neck] v4.1.1
The legendary Motown flatwound sound (James Jamerson / Pino Palladino). Fat, pillowy sub-bass thump with rolled-off highs that glues the groove together.

05. Precision Dub [Neck] v4.1.1
Ultra-deep vintage '50s dub spec. Pure, earth-shaking sub-bass thump with zero top-end click—perfect for reggae, dub, hip-hop, and classic R&B.


Music Man StingRay Family

06. StingRay Parallel [Bridge] v4.1.1
The iconic Music Man StingRay sound: thunderous low end, aggressive metallic slap clank, and that hollow midrange scoop heard on thousands of hit records.

07. StingRay Series [Bridge] v4.1.1
StingRay wired in series for a hotter, thicker signal with punchy low-mids and an aggressive, in-your-face rock bark.


Dual Soapbar Family

08. Soapbar Pair [Parallel] v4.1.1
Dual passive soapbars in parallel with active 3-band buffer (Ibanez SR / Yamaha TRBX / Sire F10). Modern articulate punch, wide dynamic range, and scooped slap clarity.

09. Soapbar Neck [Neck] v4.1.1
Solo neck soapbar humbucker with active buffer. Warm, woody, and punchy with rich low-mids.

10. Soapbar Bridge [Bridge] v4.1.1
Solo bridge soapbar humbucker with active buffer. Tight, articulate, mid-forward bite with fast attack.


P/J Bass Family

11. PJ Active [Parallel] v4.1.1
Modern active P/J punch (Sadowsky/Spector style). Combines thick split-coil body with bridge single-coil cut, boosted by a powerful active 2-band EQ.

12. PJ Passive [Parallel] v4.1.1
Classic '80s rock P/J tone (Fender Precision Special / Yamaha BB). Thick hard-rock punch with aggressive midrange bite and authoritative low-end grunt.


P/MM Modern Hybrids

13. P∕MM Parallel [Parallel] v4.1.1
Modern boutique session hybrid (Sandberg California / Lakland style). Blends the warm body of a P-bass neck with the punchy slap bite of a Music Man bridge humbucker.

14. P∕MM Series [Parallel] v4.1.1

Progressive & Classic Rock Legends

15. Rickenbacker Clank [Bridge] v4.1.1
The quintessential prog-rock clank (Chris Squire / Geddy Lee). Tight, lean low end with biting, aggressive pick attack and cutting upper-mid grit.

16. Rickenbacker Open [Bridge] v4.1.1
Modern 4003 bridge tone with the vintage capacitor bypassed. Fuller low-end body and punch while retaining that signature biting Rickenbacker top-end cut.

17. Dingwall Bridge [Bridge] v4.1.1
Modern progressive metal tone: razor-sharp pick clank, extreme note clarity, and piano-like low-B definition tailored for drop tunings and heavy distortion.

18. Dingwall Middle [Neck] v4.1.1
FD3 middle pickup solo: focused, aggressive low-mid grunt and punchy growl with exceptional articulation across all registers.

19. Dingwall Parallel [Parallel] v4.1.1
FD3 bridge and middle coils in parallel: modern scooped aggression with ultra-fast transient attack, tight sub-bass, and pristine harmonic sparkle.

The legendary Gibson "Mudbucker": a dark, colossal wall of pure vintage bass rumble with zero top-end harshness—pure '60s and '70s British blues-rock.

Jazz Bass Family
