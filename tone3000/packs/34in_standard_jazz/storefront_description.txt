ALLOMORPH: 34" STANDARD JAZZ BASS EDITION
=========================================

Transform your 34" Standard Jazz Bass into 22 legendary active, vintage, modern, and acoustic pickup configurations.

OVERVIEW
--------
Allomorph turns your 34" Standard Jazz Bass into a versatile tonal chameleon.
Instead of modeling an amplifier or speaker cabinet, Allomorph sits at the very beginning
of your signal chain (Block 1 on the Darkglass Anagram) to reshape the actual sound,
pickup response, and feel of your instrument.

RECOMMENDED SIGNAL CHAIN
------------------------
[34" Standard Jazz Bass]
  -> [Block 1: Allomorph NAM Preamp]  (High-Impedance Pickup Front-End)
  -> [Block 2: Amp / Drive / Preamp]   (Darkglass B7K, SVT, B-15, etc.)
  -> [Block 3: Speaker Cabinet IR]     (8x10, 4x10, 1x15, etc.)
  -> [FOH / Audio Interface / DAW]

QUICK INSTRUMENT SETUP
----------------------
• Master Tone Knob: 100% (Wide Open across all models)
• Pickup Selector: Set your bass switch to match the bracketed tag:
   - [Neck]: Select Jazz Neck Single-Coil
   - [Bridge]: Select 60s Jazz Bridge Single-Coil
   - [Parallel]: Select Jazz Neck + Bridge Parallel
   - [Neck]: Select Modern Active Jazz Single-Coil (Neck)
   - [Bridge]: Select Modern Active Jazz Single-Coil (Bridge)
   - [Parallel]: Select Modern Active Jazz Pair (Neck + Bridge)
   - [Bridge]: Select Jazz Jaco Bridge Growl Bias (Neck 75%, Bridge 100%)
