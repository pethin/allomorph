ALLOMORPH: ACTIVE EMG BASS EDITION

Transform your 34" active EMG soapbar bass into 26 legendary vintage passive, modern active, progressive rock, and acoustic pickup configurations.


OVERVIEW

Allomorph turns your active EMG soapbar bass into a versatile tonal chameleon. Instead of modeling an amplifier or speaker cabinet, Allomorph sits at the very beginning of your signal chain (Block 1 on the Darkglass Anagram) to reshape the actual sound, pickup response, and feel of your instrument.

Active EMG soapbars are world-famous for their whisper-quiet operation, punchy low-mids, and razor-sharp pick attack in metal and rock. But when you need the warm, organic woody body of a vintage Fender, the hollow slap clank of a StingRay, or the woody resonance of an upright, their internal active buffering can sound overly sterile and compressed. Allomorph bridges that gap, giving your active EMG bass:
• Classic Precision Bass warmth, woody low-mids, and Motown flatwound thump
• The iconic scoop of vintage '60s Jazz Basses and singing Jaco bridge growl
• Thunderous Music Man StingRay humbucker punch, hollow slap scoop, and series bark
• Modern active slap preamps, boutique P/MM hybrids, and cutting progressive rock clank
• Acoustic upright double bass resonance for jazz and acoustic sessions
• Authentic vintage passive dynamics and organic tube-amp feel, replacing active EMG highs

Calibrated for 34" Standard Scale Active EMG Basses (Spector Euro, ESP LTD, Schecter Hellraiser/Riot, Dean Edge, Jackson Pro) equipped with dual active EMG soapbars (EMG 35/40/45 series) and internal low-noise buffers. Full wave-speed modeling down to 30.87 Hz ensures seamless calibration across 4, 5, or 6-string instruments (down to low B). Plug in, select a model, and hear your active EMG bass deliver authentic vintage and boutique character in real time with zero latency.


RECOMMENDED SIGNAL CHAIN

[Active EMG Bass]
  -> [Block 1: Allomorph Pickup Twin] (Input / Pickup Front-End)
  -> [Block 2: Amp / Drive / Preamp]  (Darkglass B7K, SVT, B-15, etc.)
  -> [Block 3: Speaker Cabinet IR]    (8x10, 4x10, 1x15, etc.)
  -> [FOH / Audio Interface / DAW]

Why Block 1? Overdrive pedals and amps react directly to the resonant character of your pickups. Putting Allomorph first ensures downstream pedals and amp models distort the authentic growl of a P-bass split-coil, the clank of a StingRay, or the scoop of dual single-coils, rather than a generic active DI.


QUICK INSTRUMENT SETUP

For each voicing, set your bass's physical pickup blend knob to the recommended bracketed setting [Parallel], [Neck], or [Bridge]:

• [Parallel]: Pickup Blend at Center Detent (Both EMG Soapbars Active)
• [Neck]: Pickup Blend 100% Neck (Solo Neck EMG Soapbar)
• [Bridge]: Pickup Blend 100% Bridge (Solo Bridge EMG Soapbar)
• Onboard Active EQ (Bass, Mid, Treble): Set to Center Detents (Flat / 0 dB)

Plug straight in. All pickup positioning, pot loading, tone capacitor shaping, and active preamp EQs are handled automatically by the models.


THE 26 DIGITAL TWIN VOICINGS


Precision Bass & Super P Family

01. Precision Vintage [Neck]
The gold standard: a vintage 1962 Precision Bass with tone wide open. Warm, woody low-mids, open harmonic bloom, and organic touch sensitivity that sits perfectly in any mix.

02. Precision Active [Neck]
Fender American Ultra II active split-coil P-bass tone with 18V 3-band active preamp. Punchy, articulate, and wideband with authoritative low-end weight and bright transient clarity—built for modern rock, gospel, and mix cutting authority.

03. Dingwall Super P [Neck]
Dingwall SP1 multi-scale (32"–35") passive Neodymium Dual-P split-coil pickup. Combines fanned-fret low-end tightness with a focused 3.0 kHz passive Neodymium presence peak that cuts through dense mixes without an active preamp.

04. Precision Warm [Neck]
The legendary Motown flatwound sound (James Jamerson / Pino Palladino). Fat, pillowy sub-bass thump with rolled-off highs that glues the groove together.


Jazz Bass Family

05. Jazz Pair Open [Parallel]
The golden-era '60s Jazz Bass sound. Both pickups wide open deliver that iconic hollow midrange scoop, warm low-mids, and smooth, singing top-end.

06. Jazz Pair Active [Parallel]
The quintessential NYC active '70s-spacing slap tone: tight, massive sub-bass and crisp, glassy highs with an upward-shifted '70s comb-filter scoop that pops right out of a dense mix.

07. Jazz Pair Series [Parallel]
Fender S-1 / 4-way series mod wiring both Jazz coils in series (15.4 kΩ total, 7.6 H). Eliminates the parallel mid-scoop for a hotter, thicker humbucker-like midrange roar with deep passive inductor roll-off.

08. Jazz Bridge Growl [Bridge]
The unmistakable Jaco Pastorius tone: solo bridge pickup with authentic neck-pot decoupling under the hood for that signature singing, vocal bridge growl and burp.

09. Jazz Neck Warm [Neck]
Solo '60s neck single-coil. Deep, warm, and round with a woody low-end bloom that rivals a P-bass while retaining single-coil openness.


Music Man StingRay Family

10. StingRay Parallel [Bridge]
The iconic Music Man StingRay sound: thunderous low end, aggressive metallic slap clank, and that hollow midrange scoop heard on thousands of hit records.

11. StingRay Series [Bridge]
StingRay wired in series for a hotter, thicker signal with punchy low-mids and an aggressive, in-your-face rock bark.


P/MM Modern Hybrids

12. P∕MM Parallel [Parallel]
Modern boutique session hybrid (Lakland 44-02 / Sandberg California style). Blends the woody warmth of a Split-P neck with the scooped slap bite and sparkling top-end of a parallel Music Man bridge humbucker through an active 3-band preamp.

13. P∕MM Series [Parallel]
Modern active P/MM hybrid with the bridge humbucker switched to series coils. Delivers a punchy, mid-forward humbucker roar and muscular rock authority through the active 3-band preamp.


Rickenbacker 4001/4003 Family

14. Rickenbacker Clank [Bridge]
The quintessential prog-rock clank (Chris Squire / Geddy Lee). Tight, lean low end with biting, aggressive pick attack and cutting upper-mid grit.

15. Rickenbacker Vintage [Parallel]
Authentic vintage 4001 dual-pickup circuit with the 4.7nF high-pass capacitor engaged on the bridge pickup. Combines full-range neck sub-bass with biting bridge clank while eliminating 500 Hz phase cancellation.

16. Rickenbacker Neck [Neck]
Solo 4001/4003 "Toaster / Hi-Gain" neck single-coil positioned at the 24th-fret harmonic node (210 mm). Scoops the 4th harmonic for a deep, woody fundamental while retaining a glassy 2.34 kHz roundwound presence peak (Paul McCartney / Al Cisneros).


Dingwall Multi-Scale Family

17. Dingwall Bridge [Bridge]
Modern progressive metal tone: razor-sharp pick clank, extreme note clarity, and piano-like low-B definition tailored for drop tunings and heavy distortion.

18. Dingwall Parallel [Parallel]
FD3 bridge and middle coils in parallel: modern scooped aggression with ultra-fast transient attack, tight sub-bass, and pristine harmonic sparkle.

19. Dingwall Series [Parallel]
FD3 bridge and middle coils wired in series (Rotary Position 3): massive dual-pickup series humbucker midrange roar with fanned-fret low-end tightness and non-linear core compression.


Gibson Mudbucker & Hollow-Body Family

20. Mudbucker Deep [Neck]
The legendary solid-body Gibson EB-0 "Mudbucker": a dark, colossal wall of pure vintage bass rumble with zero top-end harshness—pure '60s and '70s British blues-rock.

21. Mudbucker Hollow [Neck]
1958 Gibson EB-2 semi-hollow maple archtop with neck Sidewinder Mudbucker: combines deep 30H inductor sub-bass with warm 165 Hz semi-hollow air-cavity bloom and rapid woody sustain decay.

22. Mudbucker Baritone [Neck]
Authentic Gibson EB-2 semi-hollow Baritone choke filter (2.5H inductor + 22nF capacitor). Attenuates sub-bass mud below 200 Hz while retaining semi-hollow acoustic resonance for a snarling, vocal midrange rock bark (Jack Bruce / Chas Chandler).

23. Hofner Violin Warm [Neck]
1962 Höfner 500/1 Violin Bass fully hollow spruce/maple archtop with neck "Staple" humbucker: iconic 145 Hz hollow-body wood bloom, rapid thumpy sustain envelope, and warm Beatle-bass midrange.


Acoustic Transducers

24. Upright Piezo [Neck]
Acoustic upright double bass: transforms your electric bass into a woody, resonant acoustic upright with natural hollow-body sustain decay, body thump, and organic finger feel. Pair with 3 Sigma Audio "Acoustic Upright Standard" AST IRs in your cabinet loader for authentic soundboard acoustic bloom.


P/J Bass Family

25. PJ Active [Parallel]
Modern active P/J punch (Fender American Ultra II style). Combines Ultra II Noiseless split-coil body with Noiseless bridge single-coil cut, boosted by the 18V 3-band active preamp.

26. PJ Passive [Parallel]
Classic '80s rock P/J tone (Fender Precision Special / Yamaha BB). Thick hard-rock punch with aggressive midrange bite and authoritative low-end grunt.
