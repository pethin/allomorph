ALLOMORPH: MUSTANG P/J BASS EDITION

Transform your short-scale Fender Mustang P/J Bass into 26 legendary active, vintage, modern, hollow-body, and acoustic pickup configurations.


OVERVIEW

Allomorph turns your short-scale Fender Mustang P/J Bass into a versatile tonal chameleon. Instead of modeling an amplifier or speaker cabinet, Allomorph sits at the very beginning of your signal chain (Block 1 on the Darkglass Anagram) to reshape the actual sound, pickup response, and feel of your instrument.

Short-scale basses are comfortable and fast to play, but often lack the string tension, low-end authority, and harmonic snap of a full-size 34" bass. Allomorph bridges that gap, giving your Mustang:
• The full, punchy string snap and harmonic bloom of standard 34" P-Basses and '60s/'70s Jazz Basses
• Thunderous Music Man StingRay humbucker punch, hollow slap scoop, and series bark
• Modern active slap preamps, boutique P/MM hybrids, and cutting multi-scale progressive rock clank
• Warm Motown flatwound thump, 1960s Hofner hollow-body violin bass, and Gibson EB-2 semi-hollow rumble
• Acoustic upright double bass resonance for jazz and acoustic sessions
• Pure studio buffer clarity or organic vintage tube-friendly passive warmth

Calibrated specifically for a 30" Short-Scale Fender Mustang P/J Bass with the 3-way toggle in the Middle position (both Split-P neck and single-coil J bridge pickups active in parallel). Plug in, select a model, and hear your short-scale bass deliver full-size 34" authority in real time with zero latency.


RECOMMENDED SIGNAL CHAIN

[Mustang P/J Bass]
  -> [Block 1: Allomorph Pickup Twin] (Input / Pickup Front-End)
  -> [Block 2: Amp / Drive / Preamp]  (Darkglass B7K, SVT, B-15, etc.)
  -> [Block 3: Speaker Cabinet IR]    (8x10, 4x10, 1x15, etc.)
  -> [FOH / Audio Interface / DAW]

Why Block 1? Overdrive pedals and amps react directly to the resonant character of your pickups. Putting Allomorph first ensures downstream pedals and amp models distort the authentic scoop of a Jazz pair, the clank of a StingRay, or the bite of an active preamp, rather than a generic DI signal.


QUICK INSTRUMENT SETUP

• 3-Way Pickup Toggle Switch: Middle Position (Both Pickups Active in Parallel)
• Master Volume Knob: 100% (Wide Open)
• Master Tone Knob: 100% (Wide Open)

Keep your toggle switch in the middle position for all 26 models—letting you switch between solo neck, solo bridge, and dual-pickup instruments completely hands-free via footswitch. All scale length scaling, aperture repositioning, pot loading, and active preamp EQs are handled automatically by the models.


THE 26 DIGITAL TWIN VOICINGS


Jazz Bass Family

01. Jazz Pair Active
The quintessential NYC '70s active slap tone: tight, massive sub-bass and crisp, glassy highs with a 53.3mm bridge-spaced midrange scoop that pops right out of a dense mix.

02. Jazz Pair Open
The golden-era '60s Jazz Bass sound. Reconstructs full 34" wide-aperture dual single-coils with that iconic hollow midrange scoop and smooth, singing top-end.

03. Jazz Pair Series
Dual Jazz single-coils wired in series (Fender S-1 / push-pull spec). Eliminates the parallel mid-scoop in favor of a hot, muscular humbucking midrange punch that drives tube amps and pedals with authority.

04. Jazz Bridge Growl
The unmistakable Jaco Pastorius tone: delivers that signature singing, vocal bridge growl and burp with full low-end body.

05. Jazz Neck Warm
Solo '60s neck single-coil. Deep, warm, and round with a woody low-end bloom that rivals a P-bass while retaining single-coil openness.


Music Man StingRay Family

06. StingRay Parallel
The iconic Music Man StingRay sound: thunderous low end, aggressive metallic slap clank, and that hollow midrange scoop heard on thousands of hit records.

07. StingRay Series
StingRay wired in series for a hotter, thicker signal with punchy low-mids and an aggressive, in-your-face rock bark.


P/MM Modern Hybrids

08. P∕MM Parallel
Modern boutique session hybrid (Lakland 44-02 / Sandberg California style). Blends the woody warmth of a Split-P neck with the scooped slap bite and sparkling top-end of a parallel Music Man bridge humbucker through an active 3-band preamp.

09. P∕MM Series
Modern active P/MM hybrid with the bridge humbucker switched to series coils. Delivers a punchy, mid-forward humbucker roar and muscular rock authority through the active 3-band preamp.


Rickenbacker 4001/4003 Family

10. Rickenbacker Clank
The quintessential prog-rock clank (Chris Squire / Geddy Lee). Tight, lean low end with biting, aggressive pick attack and cutting upper-mid grit.

11. Rickenbacker Vintage
Authentic vintage 4001 dual-pickup circuit with the 4.7nF high-pass capacitor engaged on the bridge pickup. Combines full-range neck sub-bass with biting bridge clank while eliminating 500 Hz phase cancellation.

12. Rickenbacker Neck
Solo 4001/4003 "Toaster / Hi-Gain" neck single-coil positioned at the 24th-fret harmonic node (210 mm). Scoops the 4th harmonic for a deep, woody fundamental while retaining a glassy 2.34 kHz roundwound presence peak (Paul McCartney / Al Cisneros).


Dingwall Multi-Scale Family

13. Dingwall Bridge
Modern progressive metal tone: razor-sharp pick clank, extreme note clarity, and piano-like low-B definition tailored for drop tunings and heavy distortion.

14. Dingwall Series
The signature Dingwall rotary position (Bridge + Middle FD3n pickups wired in series). Massive low-mid wall of sound with laser-focused multi-scale string tension and aggressive modern rock/metal authority (Nolly Getgood).

15. Dingwall Parallel
FD3 bridge and middle coils in parallel: modern scooped aggression with ultra-fast transient attack, tight sub-bass, and pristine harmonic sparkle.


Gibson Mudbucker & Hollow-Body Family

16. Mudbucker Deep
The legendary Gibson EB-0 solid-body "Mudbucker": a dark, colossal wall of pure vintage bass rumble with zero top-end harshness—pure '60s and '70s British blues-rock.

17. Mudbucker Hollow
1961 Gibson EB-2 / Epiphone Rivoli semi-hollow laminated maple archtop with neck Sidewinder humbucker. Combines massive 30.5" short-scale low end with warm, resonant archtop wood bloom and natural semi-hollow sustain damping ('60s soul and British Invasion).

18. Mudbucker Baritone
1961 Gibson EB-2 semi-hollow archtop with the Baritone push-button choke filter engaged (15.0H inductor + 20nF capacitor). Attenuates sub-bass mud below 200 Hz to focus a snarling, woody midrange rock bark (Jack Bruce / Chris Dreja).

19. Hofner Violin Warm
Iconic 1963 Hofner 500/1 fully hollow violin bass ("Beatle Bass") with Staple Alnico V neck humbucker, "Bass On" rhythm circuit, floating ebony bridge, and low-tension flatwounds. Explosive woody attack thump with fast, blooming hollow-body decay (Paul McCartney).


Acoustic Transducers

20. Upright Piezo
Acoustic upright double bass: transforms your electric bass into a woody, resonant acoustic upright with natural hollow-body pizzicato decay and organic finger feel. Pair with 3 Sigma Audio "Acoustic Upright Standard" AST IRs in your cabinet loader for authentic soundboard acoustic bloom.


Precision Bass & Super P Family

21. Precision Vintage
The gold standard: a vintage 1962 Precision Bass with full 34" scale tension and bloom. Warm, woody low-mids and organic touch sensitivity that sits perfectly in any mix.

22. Precision Active
Fender American Ultra II active split-coil P-bass tone with 18V 3-band active preamp. Punchy, articulate, and wideband with authoritative low-end weight and bright transient clarity—built for modern rock, gospel, and mix cutting authority.

23. Dingwall Super P
Dingwall SP1 multi-scale (32"–35") passive Neodymium Dual-P split-coil pickup. Combines fanned-fret low-end tightness with a focused 3.0 kHz passive Neodymium presence peak that cuts through dense mixes without an active preamp.

24. Precision Warm
The legendary Motown flatwound sound (James Jamerson / Pino Palladino). Fat, pillowy sub-bass thump with rolled-off highs that glues the groove together.


P/J Bass Family

25. PJ Active
Modern active P/J punch (Fender American Ultra II style). Combines Ultra II Noiseless split-coil body with Noiseless bridge single-coil cut, boosted by the 18V 3-band active preamp.

26. PJ Passive
Classic '80s rock P/J tone (Duff McKagan / Yamaha BB3000). Thick hard-rock punch with aggressive midrange bite and authoritative low-end grunt.
