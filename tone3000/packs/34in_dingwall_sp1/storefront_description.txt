ALLOMORPH: DINGWALL SP1 5-STRING (32"-35" SUPER P) EDITION
==========================================================

Transform your Dingwall SP1 5-String (32"-35" Super P) into 24 legendary active, vintage, modern, and acoustic pickup configurations.

OVERVIEW
--------
Allomorph turns your Dingwall SP1 5-String (32"-35" Super P) into a versatile tonal chameleon.
Instead of modeling an amplifier or speaker cabinet, Allomorph sits at the very beginning
of your signal chain (Block 1 on the Darkglass Anagram) to reshape the actual sound,
pickup response, and feel of your instrument.

RECOMMENDED SIGNAL CHAIN
------------------------
[Dingwall SP1 5-String (32"-35" Super P)]
  -> [Block 1: Allomorph NAM Preamp]  (High-Impedance Pickup Front-End)
  -> [Block 2: Amp / Drive / Preamp]   (Darkglass B7K, SVT, B-15, etc.)
  -> [Block 3: Speaker Cabinet IR]     (8x10, 4x10, 1x15, etc.)
  -> [FOH / Audio Interface / DAW]

QUICK INSTRUMENT SETUP
----------------------
• Master Tone Knob: 100% (Wide Open across all models)
• Pickup Selector: Set your bass switch to match the bracketed tag:
   - [Neck]: Select Dingwall Dual-P Neodymium (Neck)
   - [Bridge]: Select Dingwall FD3n Neodymium (Bridge)
   - [Parallel]: Select Dingwall Dual-P + Bridge Parallel
