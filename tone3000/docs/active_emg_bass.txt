ALLOMORPH: ACTIVE EMG BASS EDITION

Transform your 34" active EMG soapbar bass into 22 legendary vintage passive, modern active, progressive rock, and acoustic pickup configurations.


OVERVIEW

Allomorph turns your active EMG soapbar bass into a versatile tonal chameleon. Instead of modeling an amplifier or speaker cabinet, Allomorph sits at the very beginning of your signal chain (Block 1 on the Darkglass Anagram) to reshape the actual sound, pickup response, and feel of your instrument.

Active EMG soapbars are world-famous for their whisper-quiet operation, punchy low-mids, and razor-sharp pick attack in metal and rock. But when you need the warm, organic woody body of a vintage Fender, the hollow slap clank of a StingRay, or the woody resonance of an upright, their internal active buffering can sound overly sterile and compressed. Allomorph bridges that gap, giving your active EMG bass:
• Classic Precision Bass warmth, woody low-mids, and Motown flatwound thump
• The iconic scoop of vintage '60s Jazz Basses and singing Jaco bridge growl
• Thunderous Music Man StingRay humbucker punch, hollow slap scoop, and series bark
• Modern active slap preamps, boutique P/MM hybrids, and cutting progressive rock clank
• Acoustic upright double bass resonance for jazz and acoustic sessions
• Authentic vintage passive dynamics and organic tube-amp feel, replacing active EMG highs

Calibrated for 34" Standard Scale Active EMG Basses (Spector Euro, ESP LTD, Schecter Hellraiser/Riot, Dean Edge, Jackson Pro) equipped with dual active EMG soapbars (EMG 35/40/45 series) and internal low-noise buffers. Full wave-speed modeling down to 30.87 Hz ensures seamless calibration across 4, 5, or 6-string instruments (down to low B). Plug in, select a model, and hear your active EMG bass deliver authentic vintage and boutique character in real time with zero latency.


RECOMMENDED SIGNAL CHAIN

[Active EMG Bass]
  -> [Block 1: Allomorph Pickup Twin] (Input / Pickup Front-End)
  -> [Block 2: Amp / Drive / Preamp]  (Darkglass B7K, SVT, B-15, etc.)
  -> [Block 3: Speaker Cabinet IR]    (8x10, 4x10, 1x15, etc.)
  -> [FOH / Audio Interface / DAW]

Why Block 1? Overdrive pedals and amps react directly to the resonant character of your pickups. Putting Allomorph first ensures downstream pedals and amp models distort the authentic growl of a P-bass split-coil, the clank of a StingRay, or the scoop of dual single-coils, rather than a generic active DI.


QUICK INSTRUMENT SETUP

For each voicing, set your bass's physical pickup blend knob to the recommended bracketed setting [Parallel], [Neck], or [Bridge]:

• [Parallel]: Pickup Blend at Center Detent (Both EMG Soapbars Active)
• [Neck]: Pickup Blend 100% Neck (Solo Neck EMG Soapbar)
• [Bridge]: Pickup Blend 100% Bridge (Solo Bridge EMG Soapbar)
• Onboard Active EQ (Bass, Mid, Treble): Set to Center Detents (Flat / 0 dB)

Plug straight in. All pickup positioning, pot loading, tone capacitor shaping, and active preamp EQs are handled automatically by the models.


THE 22 DIGITAL TWIN VOICINGS


Precision Bass & Tone Shaper Family

01. Precision Vintage [Neck]
The gold standard: a vintage 1962 Precision Bass with tone wide open. Warm, woody low-mids, open harmonic bloom, and organic touch sensitivity that sits perfectly in any mix.

02. Precision Active [Neck]
Modern active ceramic split-coil P-bass tone with 2-band preamp. Punchy, articulate, and wideband with authoritative low-end weight and bright transient clarity—built for modern rock, gospel, and mix cutting authority.

03. Precision Mids [Neck]
Vintage P-bass with a modern vocal low-mid punch. Cuts finger clatter and high-end sizzle while keeping a muscular, forward presence in a busy mix.

04. Precision Warm [Neck]
The legendary Motown flatwound sound (James Jamerson / Pino Palladino). Fat, pillowy sub-bass thump with rolled-off highs that glues the groove together.

05. Precision Dub [Neck]
Ultra-deep vintage '50s dub spec. Pure, earth-shaking sub-bass thump with zero top-end click—perfect for reggae, dub, hip-hop, and classic R&B.


Jazz Bass Family

06. Jazz Pair Open [Parallel]
The golden-era '60s Jazz Bass sound. Both pickups wide open deliver that iconic hollow midrange scoop, warm low-mids, and smooth, singing top-end.

07. Jazz Pair Mids [Parallel]
Jazz Bass pair with a vocal midrange punch. Thickens up your fingerstyle attack and smooths out harsh fret clank while keeping every note articulate.

08. Jazz Pair Active [Parallel]
The quintessential NYC active slap tone: tight, massive sub-bass and crisp, glassy highs with a scooped midrange that pops right out of a dense mix.

09. Jazz Bridge Growl [Bridge]
The unmistakable Jaco Pastorius tone: solo bridge pickup with authentic neck-pot decoupling under the hood for that signature singing, vocal bridge growl and burp.

10. Jazz Bridge Open [Bridge]
Solo '60s bridge single-coil. Tight, laser-focused midrange bite and percussive attack, perfect for 16th-note funk and fast staccato fingerstyle.


Music Man StingRay Family

11. StingRay Parallel [Bridge]
The iconic Music Man StingRay sound: thunderous low end, aggressive metallic slap clank, and that hollow midrange scoop heard on thousands of hit records.

12. StingRay Series [Bridge]
StingRay wired in series for a hotter, thicker signal with punchy low-mids and an aggressive, in-your-face rock bark.


P/MM Modern Hybrids

13. P∕MM Parallel [Parallel]
Modern boutique session hybrid (Sandberg California / Lakland style). Blends the warm body of a P-bass neck with the punchy slap bite of a Music Man bridge humbucker.

14. P∕MM Series [Parallel]
P-bass neck and Music Man bridge wired in series for a massive wall of sound with immense low-mid thrust and heavy overdrive sustain.


Progressive & Classic Rock Legends

15. Rickenbacker Clank [Bridge]
The quintessential prog-rock clank (Chris Squire / Geddy Lee). Tight, lean low end with biting, aggressive pick attack and cutting upper-mid grit.

16. Dingwall Bridge [Bridge]
Modern progressive metal tone: razor-sharp pick clank, extreme note clarity, and piano-like low-B definition tailored for drop tunings and heavy distortion.

17. Mudbucker Deep [Neck]
The legendary Gibson "Mudbucker": a dark, colossal wall of pure vintage bass rumble with zero top-end harshness—pure '60s and '70s British blues-rock.


Acoustic Transducers

18. Upright Acoustic [Neck]
Acoustic upright double bass: transforms your electric bass into a woody, resonant acoustic upright with natural body thump and organic finger feel. Pair with 3 Sigma Audio "Acoustic Upright Standard" AST IRs in your cabinet loader for authentic soundboard acoustic bloom.


P/J Bass Family

19. PJ Active [Parallel]
Modern active P/J punch (Sadowsky/Spector style). Combines thick split-coil body with bridge single-coil cut, boosted by a powerful active 2-band EQ.

20. PJ Passive [Parallel]
Classic '80s rock P/J tone (Fender Precision Special / Yamaha BB). Thick hard-rock punch with aggressive midrange bite and authoritative low-end grunt.


Studio Buffers & Dynamics

21. Studio Passive
Studio Passive: turns your active EMG bass into a warm, organic passive instrument with natural vintage tube-amp dynamics, woody low-mid body, and smooth, rolled-off highs.

22. Studio Active
Studio Active Buffer: removes cable drag and pot loading, opening up pristine hi-fi clarity, ultra-fast transient attack, and airy top-end sparkle.
