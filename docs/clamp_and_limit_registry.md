## 2. Evaluated & Verified Forward Digital Twin Invariants

The following mechanisms have been audited and verified across the Forward Digital Twin catalog:

1. **Spatial Bridge Excursion Limiter (`alg4`, CL-04):**
   Unified partition-of-unity algebraic formulation: $\Delta G_{\text{soft}} = dg / (1 + (dg / g_{\text{eff}})^4)^{0.25}$ with $g_{\text{eff}} = \sigma(dg) \cdot 12 + (1 - \sigma(dg)) \cdot 16$ and $\sigma(dg) = 0.5(1 + \tanh(dg / 4.0))$. Eliminates piecewise boundary kinks, allowing Mudbucker neck ($+9.11\text{ dB}$) and close bridge pickups ($-9.11\text{ dB}$) to retain authentic fundamental standing-wave excursion via the $220\text{ Hz}$ low-shelf $H_{\text{pos}}(f)$ with zero artificial high-frequency counter-shelves.
2. **Small-Signal Linearity Bypass Threshold (CL-10):**
   ```python
   max_in = float(np.max(np.abs(x)))
   if max_in <= 0.10:
       return x.copy()
   ```
   Enforced across `apply_oversampled_saturation`, `apply_magnetic_saturation` (`saturation.py:255, 429`), `apply_body_sustain_sink` (`saturation.py:674`), `apply_active_pickup_dynamics` (`saturation.py:701`), and piezo bridge force compliance (`forward.py:177`), guaranteeing bit-exact $0.00\text{ dB}$ linear transfer for small-signal sweeps and pianissimo decay tails ($\|x\|_\infty \le 0.10$) while allowing forte plucks to engage physical saturation.
3. **Multi-Rate Anti-Aliasing Decimation Mask (CL-21):**
   $C^\infty$ lowpass filter mask evaluated via $1.0 - S_\infty((f - 22\text{ kHz}) / 2\text{ kHz})$ over $[22\text{ kHz}, 24\text{ kHz}]$ combined with exact $1/m$ polyphase decimation normalization (`out = np.fft.irfft(Y_down, n_sig) / float(m)`). Preserves $100\%$ flat passband through $20\text{ kHz}$ while vanishing all derivatives identically at $22\text{ kHz}$ and Nyquist ($24\text{ kHz}$), eliminating $O(1/n^2)$ algebraic boundary truncation leakage and guaranteeing $0.00\text{ dB}$ gain parity across `oversample = 1, 2, 4`.
4. **Dynamic Lenz Saturation Core Drag (CL-22):**
   Evaluated in ODE state loops via $C^\infty$ softplus excess ($\alpha=16.0$) with $V_{\text{sat}} \cdot \tanh(\text{excess}_{\text{raw}}/V_{\text{sat}})$, Charbonnier pseudo-norm ($\sqrt{x^2 + \epsilon^2} - \epsilon$), and strictly positive rational velocity drag $1.0 / (1.0 + \dots \cdot \text{excess})$. Eliminates $C^0$ conditional slope kinks (`if e > vsat:`) while ensuring smooth differentiability across the core saturation boundary.
5. **Homomorphic Real-Cepstrum FIR Synthesis Tail Window & Power Floor (CL-23):**
   Cepstral power regularization floor $\epsilon = 10^{-12}$ ($-120\text{ dB}$) matches the visualizer's $10^{-6}$ amplitude floor so deep tone-rolled passive circuits (`precision_dub`, `jazz_neck_warm`) retain their authentic stopband roll-off without premature $-80\text{ dB}$ shelving. Tail windowing across the final 15% of FIR taps uses $1.0 - S_\infty(t)$, vanishing all boundary derivatives at the window transition ($t=0$) and buffer end ($t=1$).
6. **True-Peak PCM Linear Peak Scaling (CL-16 & CL-28):**
   Peak-based linear whole-file scaling (`filtered * (CALIBRATION_PEAK_CEILING / tp)` in `dsp/conditioning.py:177`) is paired with the calibrated $-20.50\text{ LUFS}$ excitation reference standard (providing $> 20.4\text{ dB}$ crest factor headroom), guaranteeing that 100% of target voicings pass through cleanly below $-0.09\text{ dBFS}$ ($0.9900$) with zero double-compression.
7. **Luthier Individual Pickup Isolation Leveling (CL-26):**
   Models authentic luthier setup in multi-pickup blends (raising bridge pickups closer to strings to compensate for fractional standing-wave displacement $\eta = x/L$). Formulated as a smooth, shifted softplus ($\alpha = 2.0$) with asymptotic $\tanh$ ceiling ($M = 6.0\text{ dB}$) and mean-normalized across active branches ($\tilde{K}_{\text{iso}, i} = K_{\text{iso}, i} / \overline{K}_{\text{iso}}$) in composite blends (`len(active_pickups) >= 2`).
8. **Schedule-Driven Natural Convergence Architecture (CL-30):**
   Deterministic Cosine Annealing learning rate schedule ($T_{\max} = 115$ epochs, warmup floor $100$ epochs, $\eta_{\min} = 10^{-6}$) paired with Anti-Drift Pareto early stopping on composite validation loss (`mse_weight * mse + fir_hp_weight * fir_hp`, `drift_tolerance=0.15`, `drift_penalty_gain=2.5`, `drift_exit_threshold=0.25`).
9. **Dynamic Hardware Execution Tuning (CL-31):**
   Auto-resolves batch size ($8/16/32$), precision (`bf16-mixed`, `16-mixed`, `32-true`), and zero-copy in-VRAM slicing based on detected accelerator and VRAM headroom.
10. **Saddle Witness-Point Boundary Coupling Mollifier (CL-32):**
    Smoothly activates close-bridge saddle stiffness boost ($f_{\text{saddle}} = 2.4\text{ kHz}$) over $[35\text{ mm}, 75\text{ mm}]$ via $w_{\text{prox}} = 1.0 - S_\infty((x - 0.035)/0.040)$ (`physics/aperture.py:47, 63`), vanishing all spatial derivatives at $35\text{ mm}$ and $75\text{ mm}$.
11. **Fret Contact Buzz Softplus Nonlinearity (CL-33):**
    Models string-to-fretwire kinematic collision on negative string excursions for forte plucks ($A \ge 0.75$) via $C^\infty$ softplus $f_c(t) = \frac{1}{12}\text{logaddexp}(0, -12\sin(2\pi f_0 t))$ (`dsp/plucks.py:166, 281, 294`).
12. **Farina Deconvolution Tikhonov Regularization & Gate Fade (CL-34):**
    Stabilizes spectral inversion in `deconvolve_log_sweep` (`dsp/sweeps.py:102, 112`) with $\epsilon = 10^{-12}\max|X|^2$ ($-120\text{ dB}$) and tapers gated impulse tails via a 64-sample reversed $S_\infty(t)$ window.
13. **$C^\infty$ SiLU-Gated LeakyReLU Activation Homotopy & Tikhonov Warm-Start (CL-35):**
    Replaces the $C^0$ slope discontinuity of `LeakyReLU(0.01)` at $x = 0$ during early/mid training with the zero-centered, asymptotically exact $C^\infty$ homotopy $\psi_\tau(x) = 0.01 x + 0.99 \tau \, \text{silu}(x/\tau)$, cosine-annealing $\tau: 0.02 \to 0.0$ over the first $75\%$ of `min_epochs` and reverting to exact `LeakyReLU(0.01)` during `eval()` and `.nam` export. Paired with $1/\sqrt{23}$ residual `layer1x1` variance scaling and closed-form Tikhonov-regularized ($\lambda_{\text{rel}} = 10^{-2}$) Wiener-Hopf `head_rechannel` projection at Step 0.
14. **Dynamic Body Sustain Energy Sink (CL-36):**
    Models acoustic soundboard and air-cavity energy absorption on semi-hollow (`k_body_sink = 0.18`) and hollow-archtop (`k_body_sink = 0.30`) instruments via a dual-time-constant envelope tracker ($\tau_{\text{att}} = 45\text{ ms}$, $\tau_{\text{rel}} = 180\text{ ms}$) with Charbonnier pseudo-norm, $C^\infty$ softplus excess ($\alpha = 16.0, e_{\text{thresh}} = 0.035$), and rational energy drag $1 / (1 + k_{\text{body\_sink}} \tanh(\text{excess} / e_{\text{thresh}}))$ (`saturation.py:617, 657`). Preserves $100\%$ of the initial $10\text{--}35\text{ ms}$ woody pluck transient while accelerating sustain decay, and bypasses with bit-exact linearity for small signals ($\|x\|_\infty \le 0.10$, **CL-10**).
15. **Unlucky Loss Spike Detection & Automatic Training Restart (CL-37):**
    Detects catastrophic stochastic gradient explosions (`evaluate_loss_spike_condition` in `trainer/callbacks.py:228`) when `train_loss_epoch` or `mb_val` spikes to $\ge 500.0\times$ its running minimum with an absolute floor of $\ge 0.02$ (ignoring normal transient fluctuations around $10^{-4}\text{--}10^{-3}$) or becomes non-finite (`NaN`/`Inf`). Immediately aborts the corrupted trajectory, re-initializes the WaveNet A2 weights, closed-form Tikhonov warm-start, EMA shadow buffer, and Adam optimizer moments (`lr = lr * 0.85**attempt`), and restarts from Epoch 1 (up to `NATIVE_DEFAULT_MAX_SPIKE_RESTARTS = 3`).
