## Detailed Profile Specifications

### 01. `jazz_pair_active` (`34in_standard_jazz:pair_active` — Modern Active '70s Jazz Bass Pair)
* **Archetype:** Sadowsky NYC / Marcus Miller Active '70s Jazz Bass (Dual Single-Coil with '70s Bridge Spacing & 2-Band Boost-Only Preamp)
* **Pickup Architecture:** Dual parallel Alnico V single coils with '70s bridge spacing (Neck `neck`: $L = 3.20\text{ H}, R_{\text{dc}} = 7.20\text{ k}\Omega$ @ $155.6\text{ mm}$; Bridge `bridge_70s`: $L = 3.60\text{ H}, R_{\text{dc}} = 7.80\text{ k}\Omega$ @ $53.3\text{ mm}$; $102.3\text{ mm}$ spacing)
* **Control Harness:** Active harness with $250\text{k}\Omega$ master volume, $250\text{k}\Omega$ MN blend pot at center detent (`0.5`), and Sadowsky 2-band boost-only preamp (`sadowsky_2band`, $R_{\text{in}} = 1.0\text{ M}\Omega, C_{\text{in}} = 150\text{ pF}, R_{\text{out}} = 100\,\Omega$) providing $+3.5\text{ dB}$ @ $60\text{ Hz}$ low shelf and $+3.5\text{ dB}$ @ $3.5\text{ kHz}$ high shelf.
* **Electrical Parameters:** Parallel $L_{\text{eq}} = 1.70\text{ H}, R_{\text{dc}} = 3.75\text{ k}\Omega$. The preamp buffer isolates the coils from external cable capacitance, placing the compound loaded resonant peak at **$5,441.0\text{ Hz}$** ($Q = 1.06$).
* **Acoustic Character:** Modern NYC active slap sparkle, tight extended sub-bass weight, and upward-shifted '70s comb-filter scoop.

### 02. `jazz_pair_open` (`34in_standard_jazz:pair_open` — Vintage 60s Jazz Bass Pair)
* **Archetype:** Standard 1960s Fender Jazz Bass Dual Single-Coil Pair (Tone Wide Open)
* **Pickup Architecture:** Dual parallel Alnico V single coils (Neck: $3.20\text{ H}, 7.20\text{ k}\Omega$ @ $155.6\text{ mm}$; Bridge: $3.80\text{ H}, 7.50\text{ k}\Omega$ @ $63.5\text{ mm}$)
* **Control Harness:** Authentic vintage dual $250\text{k}\Omega$ volume pots in parallel ($125\text{k}\Omega$ net load), single $250\text{k}\Omega$ tone pot wide open, $47\text{ nF}$ paper-in-oil capacitor, $750\text{ pF}$ cable.
* **Electrical Parameters:** Combined $L_{\text{eq}} = 1.74\text{ H}, R_{\text{dc}} = 3.67\text{ k}\Omega$. MNA loaded resonance sits at **$2,817.2\text{ Hz}$** ($Q = 0.92$).
* **Acoustic Character:** Symmetrical dual-aperture phase cancellation produces a hollow mid-scoop around $640\text{ Hz}$ with classic woody low-mid bloom and open top-end.

### 02b. `jazz_pair_series` (`34in_standard_jazz:pair_series` — Fender S-1 Series Jazz Bass Pair)
* **Archetype:** Fender S-1 Switch / 4-Way Series Mod Jazz Bass Pair
* **Pickup Architecture & Harness:** Wires the '60s neck ($3.20\text{ H}, 7.20\text{ k}\Omega$) and bridge ($3.80\text{ H}, 7.50\text{ k}\Omega$) single-coils in series (`series` harness, $L_{\text{eq}} = 7.00\text{ H}, R_{\text{dc}} = 14.70\text{ k}\Omega$) into $250\text{k}\Omega$ master volume and tone controls ($47\text{ nF}$ cap).
* **Acoustic Character:** Series summation doubles output EMF ($+6.0\text{ dB}$ into core saturation) and lowers the loaded MNA resonant peak to **$1,680\text{ Hz}$**, transforming the scooped Jazz pair into a thick, mid-forward humbucker roar.

### 02c. `jazz_bridge_growl` (`34in_standard_jazz:bridge_growl` — 60s Jazz Bass Pair, Jaco Bridge Bias)
* **Archetype:** 1960s Fender Jazz Bass Dual Single-Coil Pair with Bridge-Biased Volume Pots (`bridge_vol = 1.0`, `neck_vol = 0.97`)
* **Control Harness:** Standard Fender Jazz VVT harness where each pickup feeds the wiper lug of its $250\text{k}\Omega$ audio-taper volume pot and both outer lugs tie to the shared output bus. Backing `neck_vol` off to `0.97` inserts $R_{\text{top,n}} = 25.50\text{ k}\Omega$ of series wiper resistance between the neck pickup and the shared output bus ($R_{\text{bot,n}} = 224.50\text{ k}\Omega$).
* **Electrical & Acoustic Physics:** Because the output bus is loaded by the low impedance of the bridge pickup ($L = 3.60\text{ H}, R_{\text{dc}} = 7.80\text{ k}\Omega$), the $25.50\text{ k}\Omega$ series resistance attenuates the neck pickup by $-12.76\text{ dB}$ relative to the bridge at $100\text{ Hz}$ (normalized MNA pickup weights: $81.3\%$ bridge / $18.7\%$ neck, effective acoustic center $x_{\text{tgt}} = 80.7\text{ mm}$, loaded $f_r = 2,126.5\text{ Hz}, Q = 0.85$). This asymmetric summing fills in the $500\text{--}700\text{ Hz}$ phase cancellation notch by $+3.2\text{ dB}$ into a vocal midrange growl while retaining $+2.7\text{ dB}$ more low-end fundamental body than the solo bridge pickup.

### 03. `jazz_bridge_open` (`34in_standard_jazz:bridge_open` — 60s Jazz Bass Bridge Single-Coil)
